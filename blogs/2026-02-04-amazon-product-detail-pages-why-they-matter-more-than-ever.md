Introduction: Product Detail Pages Are No Longer Just “Listings” For many years, Amazon sellers treated Product Detail Pages (PDPs) as static assets. Once a listing was written, uploaded, and indexed, attention shifted almost entirely to advertising, pricing, and inventory management. The product page existed mainly to convert traffic that had already been acquired elsewhere.
